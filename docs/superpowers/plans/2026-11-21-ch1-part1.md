# Chapter 1 Part 1 ("Into the ruins") Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build tranche 1 of chapter 1 — rooms 1-1 to 1-11, Gorge Gate to the Ruins Shrine — short, vertical, hard, solver-proven, plus the two small engine additions (the `light` ability, chapter-aware NPC scripts with Quill's chapter 1 lines).

**Architecture:** `tools/build_chapter1.py` becomes one function per room built with `room_builder.Room`, placed as a rising staircase on the world map. Solver cases per room in `tools/solver/chapter1.cases.json`; vent beats checked with replays. Engine: `Ability` gains `"light"`; an `npc` object's optional `script` prop selects its dialogue.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 builders, TypeScript, Vitest, solver (`npm run solve`), playwright-core playtest (scratchpad).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-ch1-level-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Abilities in chapter 1: double jump, wall jump, dash (no rewind, no echoes). First room keeps `abilities: "doubleJump, wallJump, dash"`, `echoSlots: 0`… **Ruling to apply:** set `echoSlots` to 0 (was 1) since echoes come in chapter 2.
- Every room ≥ 20×12; "short" = 20–30 wide, 12–24 tall; most rooms go **up**.
- Every entrance has a spawn; no crystal except where the spec says (1-8 first crystal) or in a 2-screen room.
- No blind jumps; every hazard visible from the spawn or on the way; no frame-perfect lines (natural replays).
- Room names unique game-wide; `python3 tools/check_world.py` clean.
- Gorge Gate keeps its left exit to The Overlook (`spawn=1`) and its arrival spawn (The Overlook exit targets `gorge_gate` spawn 0).
- Movement budget (tools/build_chapter0.py header): tap jump 2.6 / full jump 4.0 tiles across (2.4 high), double jump 6.8 (4.4 high), jump+double+dash ≈ 10, dash jump + double ≈ 11.4; any tall wall is climbable with neutral wall jumps; crystals refill on touch; dash gates target level-or-higher ledges.
- Decor from the ruins set (`ruin_house*`, `ruin_arch`, `pillar_tall`, `column_fallen`, `bell_fallen`, `rubble_*`, `rose_bush`, `rose_vine`, `lantern_dead`, `lantern_post_bent`, `balustrade`, `banner`, `statue`, `starbloom`) in every room; no decor hides a hazard.
- English UI; no `Co-Authored-By` in commits.

## Review Focus

1. **Climbing a tall wall to skip a beat** (neutral wall jumps) → every beat's walls are too short, thorned or crumble (solver "not without" cases per strict gate, Tasks 2–4).
2. **Respawn landing in danger** (spawn next to thorns or on a crumble slab) → every spawn on solid ground ≥ 2 tiles from a hazard (checked in `check_world.py`, Task 2).
3. **Upward exits that don't land** (popping into the next room over a pit) → every up-exit lands on ground within the UP_BOOST hop (solver goal "tile" just above the entrance of the next room, Tasks 2–4).
4. **Walking back down** a room you came up → falling back down lands at the previous room's top, not on thorns (solver case from the top spawn back to the room below, at least once per tranche, Task 5).
5. **Fast travel / map in the new rooms** → the map shows the chapter 1 rooms; Quill reveals outlines; Ruins Shrine still a travel target (Task 5 playtest).
